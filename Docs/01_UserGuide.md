# 📖 User Guide

**Target Audience:** Receptionists, Doctors, and Clinic Managers.
**Purpose:** A manual on how to use the software for daily clinic operations.

## Core Modules
* **Dashboard (Control Center):** Your daily starting point. View KPI metrics, search for patients, view the daily schedule planner, and process pending bills all from one screen.
* **Patient Management:** Add, edit, and search patient demographics.
* **Doctor Management:** Maintain the roster of active doctors and their consultation fees.
* **Appointments:** Book new visits. The system prevents double-booking and automatically flags missed appointments.
* **Medical Records (EMR):** Where doctors enter symptoms, diagnoses, and prescriptions. Once saved, these become permanent, read-only legal records.
* **Billing:** Generate invoices from completed appointments and collect payments.
* **Clinic Settings:** Manage the clinic’s branding (Name, Address, Contact Info) which dynamically updates the app's global header.

## Key Daily Workflows
1. **Booking a Walk-in:** Click "+ Book Walk-in" on the Dashboard. Select the patient (or create a new one), select the doctor, and pick a time.
2. **Conducting a Consultation:** From the Dashboard, find the scheduled patient and click "Consult". Enter the diagnosis and prescribe medicines. Clicking Save locks the record and updates the appointment status to "Completed".
3. **Processing Payments:** The Dashboard's "Billing Queue" highlights finished consultations. Click "Generate Bill", then click "Collect Payment" once the patient pays.
